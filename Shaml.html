Human-Computer Interaction | Graduation Project

Shaml+

A user-centered digital platform designed to improve the external child visitation experience for parents, children, and administrators.

Project Overview

Shaml+ digitizes the external visitation journey through connected experiences for parents, children, and administrative staff.

The Problem

A fragmented service journey

  • • Manual and disconnected procedures.
  • • Limited visibility of visit requests.
  • • Unclear communication between users.
  • • No dedicated child-friendly experience.

The Solution

One connected digital platform

  • • Visit requests, tracking, and reminders.
  • • A supportive child-focused experience.
  • • Safe communication and support services.
  • • An admin dashboard for case management.

My Role

UX Research UX Design UI Design Wireframing Prototyping Usability Testing

Tools Used

Figma FigJam Google Forms

Project Outcome

Usability testing showed that the main journeys were understandable for parents, children, and administrators, while also revealing opportunities to improve wording, guidance, and status clarity.

What I Learned

  • • Designing for multiple user groups.
  • • Translating research into design decisions.
  • • Conducting structured usability testing.
  • • Improving sensitive service experiences.
